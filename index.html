Tell me who the box is for, and what they're going through. I hand-pick from my own shelves: real, well-loved stories chosen for their moment, never off a list.

Each one gets wrapped and labelled for when it's meant to open. Then it's over to them, opened when the moment actually arrives. Not before.

Every box starts the same way: tell me the moment. From there, how much I get involved is up to you.
